# Levy and Rebate are informational and excluded from Net and the charts

A Levy is a charge taken off earnings automatically, before the money arrives (income tax, solidarity surcharge). Income is entered net of Levies, and the Levy is entered separately for information only. It is not part of Net and not part of any Report chart. We chose this over entering gross Income and subtracting Levy because Levies cannot be influenced: counted in the charts they would dominate the picture of where the money goes and hide the Expenses the user can actually reduce.

A Rebate (e.g. a tax refund) is the Levy's counterpart and is equally informational. The money actually received is entered as a separate Income of the same amount; nothing links the two, and nothing checks that a Rebate stays within the Levies on file. That check was judged not worth the added complexity and is a known limitation.
