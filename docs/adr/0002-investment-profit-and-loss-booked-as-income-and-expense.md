# Investment profit and loss are booked as Income and Expense

A Divestment always records the principal returned, never the proceeds. A gain is recorded as a Divestment of the principal plus an Income for the profit. A loss is recorded as a Divestment of the principal plus an Expense for the loss. This keeps every Holding's invested amount returning to zero after a full exit, and means gains and losses appear in the normal Income and Expense reports. The alternative, recording the actual proceeds as the Divestment, would leave stale invested amounts on Holdings after a loss.
